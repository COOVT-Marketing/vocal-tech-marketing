About Vocal Tech

Built by campaign veterans,
not by template agencies.

We started Vocal Tech Marketing because we were tired of generic lead vendors who treated Medicare the same way they treated solar. Insurance and legal verticals need specialists — so we became one.

Most of our core team has spent years on the buyer side of the table — running FMOs, agency groups, and PI firms. We know what a good transfer sounds like, what a bad script does to conversion, and how quickly a non-compliant campaign can get shut down.

That background shapes everything we do. Scripts get reviewed for CMS and TCPA language before they ever hit the dialer. Agents are trained on the vertical, not just handed a sheet. Reporting is built around the metrics buyers actually care about: intake rate, hold time, show rate, and cost per enrolled member or signed case.

We’re intentionally not the biggest shop in the space. We take on partners we can actually support at a high level, and we stay close to the day-to-day of each campaign. If something is off on Tuesday, you’ll hear from us on Tuesday — not in a monthly PDF.

How we work

A few principles we don’t compromise on.

Compliance isn’t optional

Every campaign starts with a compliance checklist. DNC scrubbing, recording disclosures, state licensing checks, and script approval happen before volume ramps. We’d rather lose a deal than risk a client’s license.

Quality over pure volume

We’ve walked away from volume deals that would have tanked quality scores. High-intent, well-qualified leads and transfers beat cheap raw volume every time — and our retention numbers reflect that.

Real people, real accountability

You’ll have a named account contact who knows your campaigns. When you call or email, you’re not talking to a ticket system. That matters when you’re mid-AEP or mid-enrollment season.

Full-funnel thinking

We don’t just generate the lead and disappear. Landing pages, form logic, CRM handoff, and post-transfer feedback loops are part of the same conversation. Broken handoffs kill ROI.

Transparent reporting

Daily or weekly performance digests with the numbers that matter to you. No vanity metrics. If a source or script is underperforming, we say so and fix it.

Long-term partnerships

Most of our clients have been with us for multiple seasons. We plan capacity, scripting, and agent training around recurring verticals so you’re not starting from zero every year.

Want to see if we’re a fit?

Tell us what you’re buying and the volume you’re targeting. We’ll be honest about whether we can help — and how.
